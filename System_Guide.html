🧭 System Guide

This guide explains how to use AIview effectively and provides tips to ensure the best interview experience.

Preparation Tips

  • Use Microsoft Edge for the best speech recognition and audio experience.
  • Wear earphones or headphones to prevent the AI voice from being picked up by the microphone.
  • Login before starting to access Dashboard, Results, and Learning Hub features.
  • Choose a quiet environment to improve speech recognition accuracy.
  • Speak clearly and at a moderate pace when answering questions.

How to Use AIview

Step 1 — Login

Click the Login button in the top right corner and enter your credentials.

Step 2 — Start an Interview

Navigate to the Home page and click Start Interview.

Step 3 — Upload Your Resume

Upload your CV in PDF, DOC, or DOCX format. AIview will analyze your resume and generate personalized interview questions.

Step 4 — AI Interview Session

During the interview:

  • The AI asks questions using voice synthesis.
  • A timer counts down for each question.
  • Your spoken response is captured through the microphone.
  • The text box displays the words captured from your speech, and you can edit them if necessary.
  • You do not need to wait for the timer to finish. Once you complete your answer, you can click the submit ">" button.

Step 5 — Interview Evaluation

After completing all questions, AIview evaluates your responses and generates feedback which is saved in the Results section.

⚠️ Troubleshooting & Common Issues

  • Microphone not working
    Ensure that microphone permissions are enabled in your browser and that the correct input device is selected.
  • Speech recognition not detecting your voice
    Try using Microsoft Edge and speak clearly in a quiet environment.
  • AI voice not audible
    Check your system audio or use earphones/headphones for better clarity.
  • Speech text appearing incorrectly
    You can manually edit the text in the answer box before submitting your response.
  • Interview stops unexpectedly after refreshing the page
    Avoid refreshing the page during an interview session as it may interrupt the process.
  • Timer running out before finishing an answer
    You can submit your answer early by clicking the ">" button.
  • Question generation or evaluation not working
    AIview uses the Gemini API model to generate interview questions and evaluate answers. Occasionally, these services may fail due to high traffic, temporary server unavailability, or API rate limits. This is more likely to occur during peak usage hours, especially at night. If this happens, please wait a few minutes and try again.

📊 Evaluation Methodology

AIview evaluates each interview response using an AI model and calculates the final interview score using a weighted scoring system. The evaluation considers both the quality of each answer and the difficulty level of the question.

Question Scoring

  • Each response is evaluated on a scale from 0 to 10.
  • The AI evaluation considers multiple criteria:
    • Technical correctness
    • Clarity of explanation
    • Depth of knowledge
    • Relevance to the question
  • The AI model also generates concise feedback describing strengths, weaknesses, and improvement suggestions.

Difficulty Weighting

Questions are assigned difficulty levels that influence how much each score contributes to the final interview result.

  • Easy: weight = 1.0
  • Medium: weight = 1.5
  • Hard: weight = 2.0

Final Interview Score

The final interview score is calculated using a weighted average based on the difficulty level of each question.

Formula:

Final Score =

( Σ (Question Score × Difficulty Weight) )
÷
( Σ Difficulty Weights )

This approach ensures that more challenging questions have a greater influence on the final result while still considering performance across all questions.

Example Calculation

  • Easy question score: 8 → 8 × 1.0 = 8
  • Medium question score: 7 → 7 × 1.5 = 10.5
  • Hard question score: 6 → 6 × 2.0 = 12

Total weighted score = 30.5
Total weights = 4.5

Final Score = 30.5 ÷ 4.5 = 6.78 / 10

For easier interpretation in the interface, the score is displayed on a 0–100 scale.

  • AI calculated score: 6.78 / 10
  • Displayed score in Results: 67.8 / 100

⚠ Limitations of AI Evaluation

AIview utilizes a large language model to analyze interview responses; however, automated evaluation systems possess inherent limitations. Consequently, the results should be interpreted as informative feedback designed to support learning rather than as a conclusive assessment of candidate competence.

  • Subjective interpretation: AI models analyze responses based on patterns in training data and may interpret answers differently than human interviewers.
  • Context limitations: The AI evaluates answers based only on the provided text and cannot fully account for external context, prior experience, or intent.
  • Potential scoring variability: Because AI models generate responses probabilistically, evaluations may vary slightly across different runs.
  • Communication bias: Candidates with clearer written communication may receive higher scores even if their underlying knowledge is similar.
  • Not a replacement for human judgment: The system is designed to assist learning and preparation for interviews rather than replace human recruiters or technical interviewers.

For these reasons, AIview is intended as a training and feedback tool that helps candidates practice interviews, understand evaluation criteria, and improve their responses.
